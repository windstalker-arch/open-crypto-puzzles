## 2. The certified boundary (what attempt() really does)

oracle.py docstring: phase-2/3 legs use `sha256(X).hexdigest()` as the password; the
Cosmic-Duality leg uses raw X + EVP-MD5. This is why the ANCHORED_SUMMARY statement
"the sha256(X) transform fails PKCS7 padding — prior line candidates UNCERTIFIED" is
true-but-narrow: it referred to specific legacy sweep candidates fed through the SHA-256
leg only. The current attempt() runs both legs and is self-test-certified against blobs
whose passwords are known ("causality"; raw + MD5 for B1_79). Therefore:

- CERTIFIED: every battery from the post-rebuild era (lead-0 sweeps, two-blob mirror,
  ISLAND GCM, mod-n/scalar/2-of-3, ASL/braille/codepage/base-N/script-keyword/BaseX/
  whole-stream families, Beaufort x2-implementations) — all run through certified
  attempt() on both funded gates with selftest rc=0 immediately prior.
- UNCERTIFIED (historic, vacuous negatives): pre-rebuild sweep exports that were fed as
  sha256(X)-transform only, and any "LCP/address" claims from community repos — those
  were separately re-certified negative (two-blob mirror battery; k_new base58 noise
  binding at null rate).
- EFFECTIVE funded-gate candidates across the two certified oracles: every closed family
  above; cumulative N on the order of tens of thousands, all 0 MATCH.
