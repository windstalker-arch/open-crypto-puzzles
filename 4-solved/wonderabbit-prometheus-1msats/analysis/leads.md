# Open leads: Prometheus

## 1. Obtain the companion puzzle programme (needs a person, low cost)

The author's own rule 6 states the programme is "intended to make the puzzle easier to solve,"
meaning the puzzle as designed assumes the solver has it. It has never been published online. The
collective has a direct incentive to share or sell it: wonderabbit.org advertises "Bring the
world's first Bitcoin performance puzzle to your event, Book Now," so re-staging bookings depend
on the same material. Emailing `emily.wonderabbit@proton.me` to ask whether the programme can be
shared or purchased is a zero-cost, high-value first step, and is ranked first because it is the
only path that turns this from an absence of ciphertext into an actual puzzle to solve.

## 2. Ask a specific yes-or-no question on Nostr (needs a person, low cost)

A precedent exists: a third party publicly asked the WONDERABBIT account on 2025-09-03 whether
the word "claw" appears in the seed. No public reply is on record, so the channel is unproven,
but it costs nothing to try again with a different, specific question.

## 3. Watch for a third staging of the show (ongoing, no cost)

The author posted on 2026-01-22 that the show "needs a new stage" and asked for venue ideas. A
new staging would produce a new companion programme and very likely new promotional material,
either of which could supply the missing ciphertext. This is a standing watch, not an action to
take now.
